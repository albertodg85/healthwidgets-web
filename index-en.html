Health Widgets

Apple Health widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen. The app reads the data you authorize and shows it on your iPhone. No accounts, no server, no ads.

Food log (Pro)

Log the calories and, if you like, the protein, carbs and fat of what you eat. Save your usual meals and log them with one tap from the “Meals” widget. The “Calorie Balance” widget shows calories eaten vs. burned (active + resting energy, as reported by Health).

Everything is stored in Apple Health and on your iPhone. No online database, no scanner, no photos: nothing leaves the device.

It is not a medical device and it does not replace medical advice. Calories are estimates.
