Health Widgets terms of use

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Health Widgets is an app by Alberto Davila. It shows the Apple Health data you authorize in widgets. Use of the app downloaded from the App Store is also governed by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

The app

The free version includes the suggested metrics and the basic styles. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: it unlocks the rest of the Health catalog, customization, history, Quick Log and the food log. The price is the one the App Store shows at the time of purchase, tax included. Apple handles payment, invoices and refunds; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Pro can be shared with Family and restored with the same Apple ID.

What it is not

It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and it does not replace professional advice. What it shows depends on the data in Apple Health. Calories and macronutrients in the food log are estimates you enter; the app does not give nutrition or weight-loss advice.
