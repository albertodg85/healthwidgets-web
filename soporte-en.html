Restore Pro

In the app, open the Health Widgets Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases” with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If Apple refunds the purchase, Pro turns off.

Health permission

Grant or withdraw it in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Health Widgets. Without permission, metrics show “No data”.

Delete what the app stores

Deleting the app removes the totals and preferences it stored on the iPhone. Your original data stays in Apple Health.
