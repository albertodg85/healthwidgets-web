Restore Pro

In the app, open the Health Widgets Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases” with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If Apple refunds the purchase, Pro turns off.

Health permission

Grant or withdraw it in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Health Widgets. Without permission, metrics show “No data”.

Food log

Turn it on in the app under Gallery → Food Log; it requires Pro. Swipe in “Logged today” to delete today's meals. Delete meals from earlier days in the Health app → Browse → Nutrition → Dietary Energy → Show All Data. Deleting a meal in Health also deletes its macronutrients.

Delete what the app stores

Deleting the app removes the totals, preferences and saved meals it stored on the iPhone. Your original data, and what you logged to Health, stays in Apple Health.
