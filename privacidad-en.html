Health Widgets privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Alberto Davila · albertodg85@gmail.com

2. Summary

Health Widgets shows your Apple Health data in widgets. Your health data stays on your iPhone. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and it does not share information with third parties.

3. What the app reads

With your permission, the app reads the Apple Health types you authorize, such as steps, active energy, distance, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and, with Pro, others such as weight, blood oxygen or blood pressure.

It writes to Apple Health only if you turn on Quick Log. Then it saves the water and caffeine you log from the widget, and nothing else. You can withdraw permission in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Health Widgets, type by type.

4. What it stores, and where

On your device only: the totals and latest readings you display (with unit and date), a short series for the charts (up to 30 days of daily values and, for cumulative metrics, the hourly pace of the previous 7 days), your preferences, and a flag if you bought Pro. It does not store individual samples or their source. Health totals are excluded from backups. The app does not put health data in iCloud.

5. What it does not do

It does not send your data to any server, sell it, use it for advertising or profiling, or include third-party analytics, ads or tracking.

6. Purchases

Apple processes the Pro purchase through the App Store. Health Widgets does not receive your payment details or your Apple Account details. Refunds follow Apple's terms.

7. Retention and your rights

Data stays on the iPhone while the app is installed and is removed when you delete the app. Your original data remains in Apple Health. Because the developer cannot access your data, you exercise control on the device. For any question, email albertodg85@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
